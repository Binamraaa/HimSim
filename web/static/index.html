Running the model…

Press ▶ Play. Colours show how thick the ice is.

Black line: the glacier outline in 2000. Red = the ice got thinner, blue = thicker.

Every year, snow falls on the glacier and some ice melts. Where more snow falls than melts, the glacier grows; where more melts, it shrinks. The height where the two balance is the snowline (scientists say equilibrium-line altitude, ELA). The lines show the yearly gain (right of zero) or loss (left of zero) at each height. Warmer air pushes the line left and the snowline up.

Grey band: the heights the glacier covers. If the snowline climbs above the band, the glacier has nowhere left to gain ice and will disappear, even if that takes decades.
